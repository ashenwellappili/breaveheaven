Our Story

Born from a Slow Morning Ritual

At Brew Heaven, coffee is not just a high-speed caffeine transaction. It is an intentional slow-living ritual. Founded with a vision to respect the long journey from soil to ceramic container, we source micro-lots globally.

Each origin variant is custom profiled on our low-emission drum roasters to balance delicate acidity and bring out intrinsic notes seamlessly. We partner directly with farming cooperatives across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Indonesia.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

eco

Sustainability First

We source exclusively from farms practicing regenerative agriculture. Our packaging is 100% compostable and our roasters run on renewable energy.

handshake

Farmer Partnerships

We pay above Fair Trade premiums and build multi-year contracts with our farmers, investing in their infrastructure and community programs.

science

Precision Craft

Every roast profile is data-driven and adjusted for humidity, elevation, and moisture content. We taste every batch before it reaches your cup.

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

19

2019 — The First Pour

Brew Heaven opens its doors at 124 Marine Drive, Colombo 4 with a 12-seat café, a single La Marzocca, and a vision to serve the best coffee in Sri Lanka.

21

2021 — Roastery Launch

We installed our first 12kg Probat drum roaster and began sourcing directly from Ethiopian natural lots and Colombian washed micro-farms.

23

2023 — B-Corp Certified

We achieved B-Corp certification, confirming our commitment to environmental performance, accountability, and transparency in operations.

26

2026 — Community Hub

Expanded to a full sanctuary with a tasting room, monthly brewing workshops, and a wholesale program serving 20+ local restaurants.
